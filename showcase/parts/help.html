Nothing here is a picture

Both surfaces are running code. The arcade glass and the phone app are live HTML, drawn at their real sizes: a 1080 by 3840 panel and a 440 by 956 phone. What you see is what the screens actually do.

The four tabs

  • Mobile app The phone, page by page, from the code on the cabinet to the replay you keep.
  • Machine screen The glass. Its page bar picks a group first, then a screen inside it.
  • Animation The phone and the glass side by side, playing one turn together, with the two motion clips underneath.
  • Design system The tokens, type, spacing and parts both surfaces are built from.

Customise is the point

Every screen is a stack of sections, and every section is drawn several ways. Customise, at the top right, opens on whatever screen is in front of you and lets you page through those designs one section at a time.

The panel has two parts. The top is this screen. Underneath, Across the showcase holds what both surfaces share: the theme, the background, the logo, the phone model and the score format. Your choices survive a reload.
