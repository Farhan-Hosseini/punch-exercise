How this works

Nothing here is a picture. Both surfaces are running code, at their real sizes.

  • Mobile appThe phone, page by page.
  • Machine screenThe glass. Pick a group, then a screen.
  • AnimationBoth surfaces playing one turn together.
  • Design systemThe parts both surfaces are built from.

Customise works per screen

Every screen is a stack of sections, and each section is drawn several ways. Customise opens on whatever screen is in front of you and pages through that screen's own designs. Under it, Across the showcase holds what both surfaces share: theme, background, logo, phone model, score format. Choices survive a reload.
