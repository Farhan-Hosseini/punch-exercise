The product

A physical arcade machine, installed in malls and entertainment venues, paired with a mobile app.

One credit buys one attempt. The player steps up and performs a single full-force strike against a padded target. A sensor measures it and returns a score. The attempt is over in under five seconds, and a camera records it for playback on the machine's screen.

The app is where attempts live afterwards: the player's profile, a reel of their recorded attempts and scores, and the people they're competing against.

Players are walk-up mall traffic, usually in groups, often with a queue behind them. They didn't leave the house planning to do this. Revenue is attempts: one attempt and a walk-away is a failure, three attempts and an app install is the goal.

What to design

The experience from the end of an attempt through to the player's phone. How many screens that takes is up to you. Design what the flow needs.

What it has to do:

On the machine

  • Present the score as an event, not a readout
  • Show the playback of the attempt (a placeholder is fine, don't go sourcing video)
  • Make the player want to go again
  • Get the attempt onto their phone. How that works is your call

On the phone

  • The player's profile and a reel of their attempts: score, video, when, where
  • The social layer: watching other people's attempts, reacting to them, feeling connected to the people who use these machines

Competition, optional

  • Who's the best. Which surface that belongs on is part of the question

Constraints

Arcade screen

  • 1080 × 3840 px, portrait
  • Physical panel is large, think 1.5 meters tall, 0.5 wide
  • Scores range from 0 to 999,999.000. At full length that's six digits, a separator and three decimals
  • No cursor, no keyboard, no mouse. Touch [IS / IS NOT] available, and the player has just used both hands
  • Viewed standing from 1 to 1.5 m, with spectators a further 2 to 3 m back
  • Loud ambient environment. Nothing can depend on audio
  • Runs unattended all day. Mounted, not adjustable

The touch line reached us with its choice still open, so it is shown as sent.

Mobile

  • Your choice of platform and frame size, just state which

Brand

Design tokens are below. They're a starting point, not a rule: extend them, replace them, or argue against them. You're the designer. Just tell us what you changed and why.

Deliverables

  • Figma file (view access is fine), frames at the stated sizes
  • One motion clip, 5 to 10 seconds, MP4 or GIF: the score reveal on the machine. Any tool you like
  • Half a page of notes: the decisions you made and why, anything you'd want to validate with users, and anything in this brief you'd have pushed back on

What we're evaluating

  • Whether the score reveal is genuinely exciting to look at
  • Whether the composition uses the screen it's actually on
  • Whether the motion has weight and timing, or is just movement
  • How you solved the machine-to-phone handoff
  • What you did with the brand, and what you'd change about it
  • Attention to detail

Timeline
