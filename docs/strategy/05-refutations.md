# Skeptic 1

Upstream: The handoff and the replay are built around one hit. The brief's buyer, and the spec's own run ticks, visit-grouped reel and \"credits per group visit\" metric, are built around the group run. If the run becomes the unit, several findings go away together: queued credits killing the replay, the carry strip keeping only the last code, \"Catch my next hits\" taking friends' hits, and earlier strikers having to scan beside the next person's swing. That means one run code with every hit's still, a replay that plays once inside the next Armed state, and links that cover a single hit. A second, separate cluster (median hits invisible, the kindness contradiction, the bell too small to see, house records that never reach the plate) comes from anchoring a linear scale to the 30-day maximum with the bell drawn as a rail mark. Anchor it to a percentile and make the plate underside the bell.

- [high] M-03 IMPACT, CHARGE, HANG and F-01 / F-02: The charge needs the score before it can launch, and the spec never says when the score arrives. It launches at a fixed time with a score-derived speed: "t 120 on: the full-bleed #EB1110 field rises" at "v0 8,642 px/s". But the exits are "If no valid reading arrives by 400 ms, go to F-01. If none by 5,000 ms, go to F-02", and the open questions only ask the sensor for "under 400 ms". Nothing is defined for a score that arrives at 121 to 400 ms. F-01 has two different triggers ("no valid reading by 400 ms" and "contact registered but below the noise floor"). F-02 at 5,000 ms can never fire, because F-01 has already taken the frame at 400 ms. A real hit whose value lands at 600 ms either gets the credit back and a score on top of it, or loses its score.
  Fix: Split the impact beat from the verdict. t 0 to 400: the jolt, base strip and camera blink, none of which need the score. The charge launches at max(400, score arrival). From 400 to 5,000 ms, a READING hold keeps the base strip pulsing and the plate empty. F-01 fires only on a value below the floor. F-02 fires only at 5,000 ms with no value. A credit is never refunded once contact cleared the floor. Moving the launch to t 400 also gives the striker time to finish the follow-through and look up (see the sightline finding).

# Skeptic 3

Upstream: The state machine treats the unit of play as a single hit whose Result is thrown away the moment the next credit arms. Everything tied to it (the replay, the QR, the code) dies at the next impact, and the fix-ups (queued credit to M-02b, the 20 s bar, a one-slot carry strip, first-keep-wins, auto-keep on scan) pile up around that choice. Make the unit a run: a run code on the glass that stays in place across consecutive credits and covers every hit in the run, with the last Result's replay playing in Z2 until the next impact.
