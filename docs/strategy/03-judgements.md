## Grafts
### Carry these into the winner

**Handoff and throughput (mostly from Proposal 4)**
- **The QR survives a new credit. It leaves on the next impact frame, not on credit insertion.** P1 pulls the QR within 300 ms of a credit, and in a group the friend inserts a credit while the player is still finding their phone. That loses claims at exactly the busiest hours. Keep the QR in the same place, full contrast, until the next impact, with P4's 4 px numberless shrinking bar (20 s maximum). After that, P1's letter-code carry-over line takes over for 60 s.
- **A worst-case throughput statement in the notes and the state machine:** the next player is READY within about 3 s of the previous impact, and a credit during the reveal is queued, never refused (P4 3,050 ms, P3's never cut, max 2,270 ms). A product lead needs this number to defend the reveal's length.
- **First to keep owns it, and the second scanner gets Ask M for a copy** (P4). This turns a group dispute into a follow, which is the first social edge in the graph. It replaces P1's weaker Not me only flow for the friend case.
- **The machine never shows an offline state to the queue; the phone tells the truth** (P4). Pair it with P3's machine-signed short token (8 chars Crockford base32) so offline keeps still work without forcing a long URL into QR version 4.
- **P3's staged upload order:** score and impact frame (about 80 KB) under 0.5 s, then a 540 x 960 proxy within about 3 s, then the master. A fast scanner never sees a spinner, and the claim page opens on something real. Add P3's 500 m App Clip location check so a posted photo of the glass cannot be kept from home.
- **P4's photo fallback copy** (No signal? Take a photo of this. / Good until this time tomorrow.). It carries expiry with no numeral.

**Groups (from Proposals 5, 3 and 2)**
- **One code covers every hit in the run, with a Mine toggle on the claim page** (P5), and a friend who scans the same code joins the session (P3's Tonight at Alif). A group of four should not have to organise four scans. The run's QR must never expose other people's unclaimed video. Show only score and time plus each person's own impact frame after they tick Mine.
- **After install, the app opens on the session view** (who hit, in order, best of the visit larger) with no onboarding carousel (P3). That is the install landing that explains itself.
- **The impact-frame still beside the QR** (Pocket the Punch). In a group nobody has to ask whose code it is.
- **Crew suggestions from co-presence, not follow buttons:** You've shared three runs with Maya. Add her? (P2). Pair it with P5's opt-in Also here that evening row. Together they are the Tuesday hook: people you actually stood with.
- **On-device blur of everyone behind the striker before upload** (P2), so the queue never ends up in a stranger's reel or on the attract loop.

**Motion (from Proposals 2 and Tower)**
- **A 50 ms hit-stop when the charge passes the TODAY line, before it snaps** (P2). It gives resistance at the moment the crowd is asking whether it will make it. It costs 50 ms and needs no numeral.
- **When a hit's charge passes the player's own earlier run tick, that tick flashes and kicks 16 px** (Tower's PASSED YOU, kept wordless and anonymous). It is the most legible improvement beat from 4 m.

**Guardrails (from Proposals 3, 2, 4 and Pocket the Punch)**
- **A wellbeing cap:** after 5 linked hits in 15 minutes the go-again block becomes GOOD SESSION. / Your hits are on your phone. and auto-linking stops (P3, P2). It protects wrists, venues and the brand.
- **A per-venue switch** for go-again pressure copy and charge brightness (P4 and P1's own risk list). Family venues and dim venues differ.
- **Keep / kept as the only verbs players see**, never claim (P3). P1 mostly does this already; enforce it.

**Business asks for the notes, off the glass**
- A bundle price (three credits) as an operator test, shown on the reader, never in the reveal (Pocket the Punch, P5's rejection of on-screen price nudges).
- A physical GO AGAIN button as the first hardware ask.
- Claim rate per run and credits per group visit as the north-star metrics, reported alongside installs per keep.

## Kill list
### Must not survive

**Manipulative mechanics**
- **The r-squared height scale** (P5). It exaggerates how close a near miss looks, by the author's own admission. The height must stay linear and true, as P1 already rules.
- **Near-miss copy of any kind:** JUST SHORT (P3), SO CLOSE / 3,120 SHORT OF MAYA (P2), ALMOST TODAY'S BEST and So close. Go again. (P4). It is the slot-machine lever, and a 3 to 5% threshold sits inside the sensor's likely repeatability, so the claim may not even be true. P1's constant hang and name-the-target-only copy replace it.
- **MAYA, RUN IT BACK and any go-again call aimed at the person who just lost the lead** (P2). It is a public spend prompt at the most vulnerable moment.
- **A visible round or set drain bar next to the go-again ask** (P2's 90 s round bar). It reads as urgency even without digits. The only allowed timer is the numberless bar on the code's own lifetime, placed away from the credit prompt.
- **The Show my handle on machine boards toggle defaulting to on** (Pocket the Punch). Visibility on public glass is opt-in, with equal-weight buttons (P2's rule).
- **Empty set slots as endowed progress** (Pocket the Punch). Unverified causal copy also goes: First one is a warm-up, Most second hits land harder, and Aim through the pad (Pocket, P4, P5). Copy like that ships only if fleet data proves it, and never as injury-adjacent coaching.
- **Loss-framed notifications:** The Alif board closes tonight. You're 4th. (P3). The notification layer stays: someone beat your mark, a challenge received, your session video.

**Public shaming and privacy**
- **Tier words stamped on every hit:** WARM-UP to MONSTER (Tower), LIGHT to BEAST (Pocket the Punch), WARM-UP (P5). A group reading LIGHT over their friend's head kills the second credit. Weak hits get silence or a neutral target line.
- **Real handles or named people on the glass during play** (Tower PASSED @LINA, P2 named lines). The glass stays anonymous; names live on the phone. The only exception is an opted-in adult record holder on the attract loop.
- **Strangers' faces on public glass:** P4's round board of impact-frame thumbnails, and any attract clip without per-clip opt-in. P1's anonymous ticks do the same job with no faces.
- **Find your hit grids of strangers' stills, even blurred** (Pocket the Punch, Tower), and P5's open set link showing unclaimed videos to anyone holding the link for 30 minutes. A lost hit is lost (P4's honest trade), or at most P3's imageless pending claim.

**Handoff geometry that breaks the physics**
- **A QR outside chest height:** Pocket the Punch at 0.73 to 0.94 m (legs occlude, phones point down), and P3's ticket at 1.52 to 1.73 m in the eye band (photo is the scan is unvalidated).
- **Off-white tiles or dark-brown modules** (P3 #EBE6E4 with #221E1D, P5 #f4efed). The rule stays #000 modules on #FFF, integer pixel modules, 4-module quiet zone, never animated while live.
- **A long offline token that forces QR version 4 at 11 px modules** (P4). Use a short signed token in an uppercase alphanumeric URL at version 2.
- **P1's own rule that the QR leaves within 300 ms of a credit.** Replaced by the leave-on-next-impact graft.
- **The Pass (a rotating phone QR held up to the cabinet camera) as a check-in path** (P2). It depends on unconfirmed camera angle and glare. NFC or in-app arming near the machine covers the same need.

**Motion and throughput**
- **Full-field red or white flashes:** P2's 90% red overlay, the Tower's and Pocket's one white plus two red frames, and a 14 Hz canvas shake on a 1.5 m panel. They are photosensitivity risks. P1's single rising charge stays, capped per venue.
- **Uninterruptible reveals longer than about 2.5 s** (P5's 2,000 ms climb plus 1,600 ms hold). A credit must be queued and READY within about 3 s.

**Scope and IA**
- **Five-tab phone IA with a Pass or Scan tab in the centre** (P2, P3, Pocket the Punch). Four tabs at most. The claim flow lives outside the tabs, as P1 has it.
- **Magenta meaning you** (P4). Red is you and the strike; magenta is only other people's marks.
- **A card feed replacing the vertical video feed** (Pocket the Punch). It is an untested bet against platform habit and adds nothing to three attempts or an install.
