          Principles

          Four rules settled most arguments while the screens were drawn. When two designs disagreed, the one that kept these won.

          Red keeps one job

          Red is the strike and what it earned: the score's plate or charge, its best rank, the replay's progress and the next move, Punch again. Nothing decorative is red, so from three metres back red always points at this hit.

          Height does the ranking

          A harder hit sits higher. The charge climbs a column on Pole, the Big score's strength tester, and the boards stand punchers on steps and bars, so a rank reads as height before anyone reads the number under it.

                          Pages and sections
